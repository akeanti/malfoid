I Meet Malfoid

Meet Malfoid

She reads your wrist like a ledger, and judges it accordingly.

Malfoid is a complete, open hardware project: firmware, circuit design, a 3D-printable body and this site. Underneath the attitude it's an embedded systems project done properly, with interrupt-driven sensing, sensor fusion, an on-device recognizer tested against thousands of simulated casts, and a power design that sleeps on microamps.

99.6%Top-1 accuracyon 2,700 deliberately sloppy synthetic casts; 0.1% accepted as the wrong spell.
70°Wrist roll, ignoredThe AHRS tracks where the tip points, so a rolled wrist draws the same shape.
One castTo teach a spellName it in the console, cast once, and it's stored on the wand.

Draws in the air

Madgwick sensor fusion at 208 Hz, with the accelerometer ignored mid-flick and gyro drift relearned whenever she rests.

Thinks on the wand

Dynamic time warping against spell templates on the ESP32-S3 itself. No cloud and no training set.

Commands your home

MQTT with Home Assistant discovery: every spell arrives as a device trigger, ready for automations.

Feels alive

An LRA haptic signature per spell, a light-piped tip, and deep sleep until an IMU interrupt notices you've picked her up.

IV Under the hood

From flick to "lights on."

Sensing runs in its own FreeRTOS task, woken by the IMU's data-ready interrupt. Everything else, from recognition to Wi-Fi and haptics, waits its turn.

  1. i

    Sense

    Gyro and accelerometer are read over I²C on every data-ready pulse.

    208 Hz · ±2000 dps · ±8 g
  2. ii

    Fuse

    Madgwick AHRS. Gyro bias is relearned at rest; the accelerometer is ignored mid-flick.

    β 0.08 · 0.85–1.15 g gate
  3. iii

    Project

    Tip direction becomes azimuth and elevation relative to where the cast began. Wrist roll cancels out.

    104 points / s
  4. iv

    Resample

    32 equidistant points. Lines keep their aspect ratio; shapes are squared up by principal axes.

    32 × (x, y)
  5. v

    Match

    Dynamic time warping in a Sakoe–Chiba band against every template. The winner must beat the runner-up.

    ≈1 ms on the ESP32-S3
  6. vi

    Act

    Tip light, a haptic signature, and an MQTT message that Home Assistant can turn into anything.

    MQTT · device triggers
# every cast, as your broker sees it
$ mosquitto_sub -t 'malfoid/#' -v
malfoid/a1b2c3/status  online
malfoid/a1b2c3/spell   lumen
malfoid/a1b2c3/state   {"battery":76, "voltage":3.93,
                        "charging":false, "last_spell":"lumen",
                        "rssi":-58}

# automations.yaml - or build it in the UI: Device → Malfoid
- alias: "Lumen turns the lights on"
  triggers:
    - trigger: mqtt
      topic: "malfoid/+/spell"
      payload: "lumen"
  actions:
    - action: light.turn_on
      target:
        entity_id: light.living_room
