Meet her

Real engineering, dressed for the gala.

Draws in the air

A Madgwick AHRS tracks where the tip points, not raw gyro axes, so rolling your wrist mid-spell doesn't smudge the shape.

Thinks on the wand

Dynamic time warping against spell templates, on the ESP32-S3 itself. No cloud and no training set, and you can teach her new spells in one cast.

Commands your home

MQTT with Home Assistant discovery: every spell appears as a device trigger, so "Lumen → living-room lights" is two clicks.

Feels alive

A linear resonant actuator on a TI DRV2605L, a light-piped tip, and deep sleep until an IMU motion interrupt notices you've picked her up.

How it works

From flick to "lights on"

Sensing runs in its own FreeRTOS task, woken by the IMU's data-ready interrupt. Everything else waits its turn.

Sense

LSM6DSOX gyro + accelerometer, read over I²C on every data-ready pulse.

208 Hz · ±2000 dps

Fuse

Madgwick AHRS. Gyro bias is learned whenever she rests; the accelerometer is ignored mid-flick.

β = 0.08 · 0.85–1.15 g gate

Project

Tip direction becomes azimuth and elevation relative to where the cast began. Wrist roll cancels out.

104 points / s

Resample

32 equidistant points, centred. Lines keep their aspect ratio; shapes are squared up.

32 × (x, y)

Match

Dynamic time warping in a Sakoe-Chiba band against every template, with a confidence margin.

~1 ms on the S3

Act

Tip light, a haptic signature, and an MQTT message that Home Assistant turns into anything.

device triggers
Hardware

Two ways to build her

Start with breakout boards and a XIAO ESP32S3 on a free afternoon, or go all the way with the Malfoid Core: an 18 × 92 mm, 4-layer board that lives in the handle.

Path A · afternoon build

XIAO + breakouts

Seeed XIAO ESP32S3, STEMMA QT breakouts for the IMU, haptics and gauge, one SK6812, and copper tape under a 3D-printed emerald. No SMD soldering.

Firmwarepio run -e xiao
Battery1S LiPo, 400–500 mAh
Asleep~1–3 mA (cut breakout LEDs: ~0.3 mA)
Path B · the real thing

Malfoid Core rev A

ESP32-S3-MINI-1, USB-C with load-sharing charger, TPS63802 buck-boost, a power-gated tip LED behind a level shifter, and the IMU near the wrist pivot.

Firmwarepio run -e core
Battery14500 Li-ion, 800 mAh
Asleep~40–60 µA estimated, ~9 weeks of typical use
Design notes Power budget 3D-printable body
